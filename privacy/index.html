BandlorePrivacy

What Bandlore records. Bandlore records audio only while you have armed a session or started a capture, and it says so on screen while it does. Recordings stay on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud database. When you share a band with other people, everything in that band — recordings, ideas, comments — is shared with them and with nobody else.

Location. If you turn on arrival reminders, Bandlore keeps the coordinates of your practice space and asks iOS to wake it when you arrive. It stores that one location and no history of where you have been. Turn reminders off in Settings and the location is deleted.

Transcripts and markers. Session intelligence is off until you switch it on in Settings › Features. Once it is on and you turn on Transcribe in Settings, on devices that support it, Bandlore can transcribe a take and suggest section markers. That work happens on your device with Apple's speech and language models; audio is never sent to us or to any third party. When transcription is switched on, a session is transcribed as soon as it stops, before anyone reviews it, so the text of everything said in the room syncs to the band's members as text until the review is complete; the audio is never part of that. Generated text is labelled as generated.

What we collect about you. Nothing beyond the above. Bandlore has no accounts, no analytics service, no advertising and no tracking. It never sells or shares data. The only data that leaves your device is what iCloud syncs to your own account and what you choose to share with your band.

Deleting your data. Deleting a session, idea, song or band removes it from your device and, once synced, from iCloud. Deleting the app removes everything local; iCloud data is removed from Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage → Bandlore.

Contact. Questions go to the address on the App Store page. Last updated 2026-11-17.
