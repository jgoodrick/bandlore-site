BandloreSupport

Get in touch. Questions, problems and ideas go to support@josephgoodrick.com. Say which device you are on and what you were doing, and you will hear back from the person who makes the app.

Recording a practice. Open a band, go to Sessions, tap ＋ and choose Session Mode, then Arm. Put the phone down; Bandlore records the room and cuts the recording into takes where the band played. Stop the session when you are done. For one quick idea, choose Rapid Takes instead.

Reviewing a session. A stopped session waits under Sessions. Open it to see its takes in the order you played them, then name the keepers, trim, split or merge them, and drop the rest. Kept takes appear under Ideas.

Sharing with your band. Create a Shared Space, invite your bandmates, and send them the link. They join by opening the link on a device signed in to iCloud. Sharing and sync need iCloud; without it Bandlore keeps everything on the one device.

Where recordings are kept. Every recording is a file on the device that made it, listed under Settings. From there you can share a recording or add it to a band.

More features. Shows, Releases, Content and the band's Books start switched off. Turn on the ones your band uses under Settings › Features; the choice applies to your device only.

Deleting your data. Deleting a session, idea, song or band removes it from your device and, once synced, from iCloud. Settings also offers Export my data and Erase everything. See the privacy note for the rest.

Bandlore is an idea-capture and session recorder for one band.
